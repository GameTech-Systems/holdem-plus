# Outside audit of the equity simulation and Strategy Guide (2026-11-25)

An outside (GPT) audit of `sim/` and the live Strategy Guide was checked finding by finding against the code, with
the simulator rebuilt and rerun, rather than accepted or dismissed as written. This is the record of what was
found and what was done. The audit text itself is not kept in the repo; the verdicts below are the useful part.

| # | Audit finding | Verdict | What was done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standard error understated | **Partly valid.** The README said "standard error"; ±0.1 is fair heads-up (max 0.091). Six-handed max is 0.129, and the guide footer said "about ±0.1 points" with no label. | Simulator outputs per-hand SE for Hold'em, Plus and the paired difference, plus shape SEs and a `meta` block. Guide tooltips, a data table and the footer show them. |
| 2 | Raw equity is not strategic EV | **Valid, already disclosed** in README and footer. | Note above the chart; "vs random hands" and "all-in EV" wording; the EV formula's assumptions stated. |
| 3 | Evaluator validation is only a random cross-check | **Valid. No evaluator defect found.** | `--selftest` (exact 5- and 7-card counts; 6/7/8-card subset consistency), `exhaustive_check.py` (all 7,462 five-card hands), `edge_cases.py` (24 hand-derived showdowns). Planted bugs showed each layer misses something another catches. |
| 4 | Third-card odds (2/47, 6/47, 11/47) misleading | **Valid, confirmed by full enumeration.** Averaged over flops the odds are exactly 2/50, 6/50, 11/50; 2/47 etc. hold only when the flop contains none of your outs. | Guide gives both, plus conditional flush odds (21%, 19%, 17%). `sim/third_card_odds.py` reproduces and asserts them. |
| 5 | `--seed 0` makes every deal identical | **Valid, and broader:** a non-numeric seed became 0 and bad trial counts printed `-nan` with exit 0. | Arguments validated, exit code 2; asserted in CI and in `run_checks.sh`. |
| 5b | Modulo bias in `rnd() % n` | **Not accepted.** About 1e-18 for n <= 50 on a 64-bit generator. | Documented in `sim/README.md`; code unchanged. |
| 6 | Separate evaluator / deal / equity validation; exact-enumeration benchmark | **Partly done.** | Layers separated and documented. Exact Hold'em Plus benchmark not built (AA vs random is about 2 billion boards); known Hold'em equities asserted within 0.5 points instead. |

Strategy-guide items: opening ranges labelled provisional, with UTG/hijack labels; no big-blind opening row (the
big blind never opens an unraised pot); cash and tournament advice qualified; set-mining rule now cites an exact
figure (pocket pair is a set or better by flop betting 1 in 6.4 here against 1 in 8.5 in Hold'em); rule of 2 and 4
given an "outs are not clean" caveat; hand tiers given a board-relative note; the "chart is not accessible" point
fixed with a collapsible data table.

Found in review, not by the audit: the guide claimed suited and connected hands gain most from the plus card (they
do not: base equity and gain correlate at -0.93; the biggest gainers are simply the weakest hands); "exact" was
wrongly applied to paired differences (pairing cuts the SE about 30%, not to zero); two shape equities depended on
C's unspecified argument evaluation order (now explicit, numbers unchanged); `T7s` was missing from the button
range; the build needed `-lm`.

## Re-verified 2026-11-25 (second pass)

From a clean clone: the simulator rebuilds with no warnings; `--selftest`, `exhaustive_check.py`,
`edge_cases.py`, `cross_check.py` (40,000 showdowns), the six invalid-argument rejections and
`third_card_odds.py` all pass; a default-seed rerun reproduces every number embedded in `strategy.html` exactly
(`check_published.py`); and the guide's prose figures (AA 85 to 75 heads-up and 49 to 35 six-handed, 72o 35 to 40,
A9o 61 to 54, 54s +7.7, 52o +7.3, correlation -0.93, 166 of 169 differences beyond 2 SE, SE reduction about 30%,
max SE 0.091 and 0.129) match the data.
